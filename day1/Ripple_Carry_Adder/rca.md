A Ripple Carry Adder (RCA) is a combinational circuit used to perform the addition of two binary numbers. It is constructed by cascading multiple Full Adders, where the carry output of each stage is connected to the carry input of the next stage. In a 4-bit RCA, four Full Adders are connected in series to add two 4-bit binary inputs along with an optional carry input. The sum bits are generated at each stage, while the carry propagates from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB), giving rise to the name "Ripple Carry Adder." The design is implemented in Verilog HDL and verified through simulation in Vivado, demonstrating correct binary addition and carry propagation for different input combinations.
